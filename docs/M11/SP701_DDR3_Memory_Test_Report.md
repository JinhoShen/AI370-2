## Limitations and status language

- **PASS:** bounded 16 MiB MIG configuration exercised physically for 30 seconds with calibration asserted, substantial write/read status activity, sticky compare/error signal low, and JTAG retained.
- **NOT TESTED:** all-address exhaustive coverage of the 16 MiB window; full 512 MiB MIG address space; full 1 GB physical SODIMM; per-pattern completion counts; numeric total error count; long-duration/temperature margin testing.
- **NOT OBSERVED:** short `test_complete`/write/read completion pulses in the 1,024-sample end-of-dwell ILA capture.
- A generated bitstream or successful calibration alone is not the basis for PASS; the physical traffic and compare observations above are required evidence.
